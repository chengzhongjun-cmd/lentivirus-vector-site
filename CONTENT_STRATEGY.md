# Content positioning and review

## Audience
- CDMO and biotech CMC teams: USP/DSP, characterization, transfer, PPQ readiness.
- Suppliers and technology providers: application fit, evaluation and collaboration.
- Research and strategic partners: targeted LVV and in-vivo CAR-T manufacturing perspectives.

## Editorial safeguards
- Content is educational; do not imply an established consulting entity or guaranteed deliverables.
- Do not disclose employer-confidential process data, internal studies, nonpublic performance metrics, or proprietary protocols.
- Verify external technical and regulatory claims against primary sources before publication.
- Screen inquiries for confidentiality, intellectual-property ownership and conflicts of interest.

## Release checks
1. Run `npm install` and `npm run build` locally or in CI.
2. Check English and Chinese homepage, mobile navigation, both hero links, collaboration CTAs, article routes and Kit subscription form.
3. Confirm any collaboration inquiry workflow is approved before public promotion.
4. Verify metadata and accessibility; search and downloadable tools remain future improvements.

## Rollback
Changes are isolated on `content/lvv-positioning-partner-pathways-20261026`. Do not merge until reviewed. Close the draft PR or delete the branch to abandon; if merged, revert the merge commit.
